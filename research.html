Co-first author · AICSIP 2026 · Hangzhou, China

LLM Performance on Standardized Examinations

Compared four language models on approximately 1,500 benchmark questions and custom AP, A-Level, Gaokao, and TMUA exam datasets. Prompt tests, full-paper simulations, and a six-part error analysis show where strong scores still hide reading, reasoning, and calculation failures—and motivate a future hybrid-model design.

Sole author · LAAI 2025 · Chongqing, China

Predictive Analytics for Adolescent Mental Health

L. Huang · 2025 International Conference on Low-Altitude Airspace and Artificial Intelligence (LAAI) · IEEE · pp. 19–25 · DOI: 10.1109/LAAI69202.2025.00010 ↗

Using academic, lifestyle, and psychological variables from a Kaggle-sourced dataset, I compared logistic regression, SVM, random forest, and XGBoost. XGBoost reached the highest reported AUC-ROC (0.9610), while other models led on accuracy or recall; the study frames prediction as support for timely human attention.
